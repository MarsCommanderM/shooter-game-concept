# Gameplay Presentation Baseline V1

This baseline is native STW C++ only. `--playtest game` runs `GameRuntime`,
`WeaponSystem`, `TargetWorld`, T4-B animation, and `GLRenderer`. The browser is
still only an authenticated-server-side transport for fixed input messages and
PNG readbacks from the native OpenGL backbuffer.

## Ownership and separation

- Ammo, cooldown, reload, switching, hitscan, damage, and fire authority remain
  renderer-free gameplay state.
- `GameplayPresentation` observes authoritative events and never creates a
  gameplay shot.
- `GameAudio` and all viewmodel/effect drawing are linked only into the native
  `stw` client target.
- `cache_builder` and the animation/runtime tests do not link SDL audio, SDL
  video, or OpenGL through these additions.
- No STWC format/version, skinning shader, animation sampler, or T4-B state
  transition was changed.

## Known presentation limits

Baseline V1 is a functional, permissibly generated training presentation, not
final game art. It has no arms/hands asset, moving or shooting bot AI,
footsteps, player health, visible sky dome, native text-rendering HUD, or audio
transport to the browser. Final visual and game-feel acceptance must be made
from the real VPS/mobile framebuffer; compilation and state tests cannot make
that claim.
