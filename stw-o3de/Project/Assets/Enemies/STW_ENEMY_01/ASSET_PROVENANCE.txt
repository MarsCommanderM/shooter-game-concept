STW_ENEMY_01 ASSET PROVENANCE
=============================

STW_ENEMY_01 is an original fictional robotic/alien combat unit generated
deterministically by tools/assets/create_stw_enemy_01.py. No external, downloaded,
paid, copyrighted third-party, real-person, or real-world military asset is used.
The static OBJ uses a text-authored Atom StandardPBR material and no textures.
Gameplay health, movement, damage, death and respawn remain in EnemyCombatModel;
the mesh and material are presentation-only.
