authored by the controlled Blender 4.5.13 headless toolchain.

The source set contains one skinned mesh and the same deterministic 18-joint
armature in each file. Motions are intentionally in-place:
  STW_CHARACTER_01.glb                 IDLE
  STW_CHARACTER_01_IDLE.glb            IDLE motion product
  STW_CHARACTER_01_LOCOMOTION.glb      LOCOMOTION
  STW_CHARACTER_01_DEATH.glb            DEATH

Stable joint contract:
  root, pelvis, spine, upper_spine, neck, head
  upper_arm.L, lower_arm.L, hand.L
  upper_arm.R, lower_arm.R, hand.R
  upper_leg.L, lower_leg.L, foot.L
  upper_leg.R, lower_leg.R, foot.R

The EMotionFX joint hierarchy is presentation data only. Future PhysX
ragdoll node indices must remain a separate mapping from these joint names.
