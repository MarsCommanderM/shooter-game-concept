The STW_ARENA_01 modular kit is original STW production geometry.

Geometry generated deterministically by tools/assets/generate_stw_arena_kit.py:
  STW_ARENA_DECK_01.obj      segmented deck plates, channels, hatches, framing
  STW_ARENA_WALL_01.obj      framed wall modules, ribs, rails, bays, vents
  STW_ARENA_STRUCT_01.obj    wall pilasters, roof trusses, tie beams
  STW_ARENA_COVERMOD_01.obj  cover modules and the step platform
  STW_ARENA_ARCH_01.obj      suspended hero gate structure
  STW_ARENA_BEACON_01.obj    landmark tower and the arena's emissive channels
  STW_ARENA_TRIMKIT_01.obj   kick plates, cap bands, hazard trim
  STW_ARENA_PROP_01.obj      set dressing: crates, barricades, conduit, housings
  STW_ARENA_MARK_01.obj      painted marking planes

Block 26E-R1 made the presentation coherent with the gameplay collision world.
Gameplay owns collision: PhysXPlayerRuntime builds exactly eight static boxes
(floor, four perimeter walls, two covers, one step) and this kit adds none. Block
26E had authored free-standing pylons, hero-arch legs and deck crates in open
floor, where no collider exists, so the player walked through them. In R1 every
solid component either sits on one of those eight colliders, stays behind the
wall plane, clears the standing capsule, or is flush enough to walk over:
  pylons  -> engaged pilasters standing in the wall relief
  arch    -> suspended from the roof trusses, underside at 1.95 m
  props   -> perimeter equipment lines, cover-top dressing, flush deck pads
tools/assets/validate_stw_obj.py enforces this per component, purely by
position, deriving the collider list and the player capsule from
PhysXPlayerRuntime rather than keeping an editable copy.

Materials and textures generated deterministically by
tools/assets/generate_stw_materials.py (eight StandardPBR families).

Geometry is authored raw Z-up, +Y forward, in metres, unrotated. O3DE imports
OBJ vertices as (-x, z, y) and the runtime applies the compensating rotation
Matrix3x3::CreateFromColumns(-X, Z, Y) at bind time.

This kit supersedes the Block 26D blockout meshes (STW_ARENA_01.obj,
STW_ARENA_COVER_01.obj, STW_ARENA_LANDMARK_01.obj, STW_ARENA_TRIM_01.obj) and
their unreferenced .mtl sidecars, all removed in Block 26E along with the
superseded tools/assets/generate_stw_arena.py.

No external, copyrighted, downloaded, or paid asset was used.
